Why list with D&D Luxury

We treat each item as our own — kept impeccable, insured throughout, returned to its owner without compromise.

  • Insurance up to R500,000 per item, every transaction, every transit.
  • In-person authentication and condition grading at no cost.
  • Every buyer and renter is verified — ID, address and references.
  • Sellers keep 80% of sale price; listers keep 75% of each rental fee.
  • Discretion as standard. Your name is never shown publicly.
