How DnD Lux works

Two sides of the same quiet exchange — meticulously orchestrated by our concierge team.

For renters

1

Discover the piece

Browse hand-picked listings. Every item is photographed, condition-graded and authenticated before it appears.

2

Reserve your dates

Choose your window. We coordinate delivery — by hand, in protective casing, anywhere within our service zones.

3

Wear it. Return it.

Enjoy the piece for your occasion. We collect, professionally clean and return it to its owner. Insured throughout.

For listers

1

Submit your piece

Upload images and provenance. Our specialists authenticate, photograph and grade the item — at no cost.

2

We list, vet and book

Your piece appears in the archive. We screen every renter — no profiles without verified ID and references.

3

Earn discreetly

Receive 75% of each rental fee. Pieces typically earn 8–14% of their value annually with negligible wear.
