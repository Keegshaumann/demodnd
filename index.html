Two ways to wear

Own it forever, or borrow it briefly.

Same authentication, same insurance, same considered handling. The choice of how long is yours.

To buy

1

Browse the archive

Every item authenticated in person, photographed in our studio, condition-graded by independent specialists.

2

Reserve or finance

Pay in full, place a holding deposit, or apply for finance over 12 to 36 months. White-glove delivery within South Africa is included.

3

Take ownership

Receive the piece by hand, with original packaging and full provenance documents. 14-day return on any authentication discrepancy.

To rent

1

Choose your dates

Reserve any rentable piece for an evening, a fortnight, or a season. Same authentication, fully insured throughout.

2

We deliver, by hand

Our courier brings the piece in protective casing. Cape Town and Johannesburg same-day; nationwide within 48 hours.

3

Wear it. Return it.

We collect, professionally clean and return it to its owner. Should you fall in love, the rental fee credits towards purchase.
