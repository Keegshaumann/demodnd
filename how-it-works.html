A members' protocol

The quiet exchange, end to end.

Every transaction on DnD Lux follows the same considered choreography — whether you are borrowing for a single evening or listing a piece you rarely wear.

01

Discover

Browse the archive of authenticated handbags, watches, jewellery and shoes. Every item is condition-graded and photographed in our studio.

02

Reserve

Select your dates, confirm identity and pay securely. Our concierge confirms availability with the lister within hours, never days.

03

Wear & return

Receive the piece by hand, fully insured. Wear it for your occasion. We collect, professionally clean, and return it to its owner.

01

Submit

Send images and provenance documents. Our specialists authenticate, photograph and grade the item — at no cost to you.

02

We vet & list

Your piece appears in the archive. Every renter is screened with verified ID, references and a security deposit before booking.

03

Earn

Receive 75% of each rental fee, paid weekly. Pieces in our care typically earn 8–14% of their retail value annually.
